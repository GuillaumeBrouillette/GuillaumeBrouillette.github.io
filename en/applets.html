Interactive applets

These applets illustrate notions of computational topology and geometry related to my research. They run directly in the browser, without installation.

Multiparameter discrete Morse theory

The algorithm GenerateMDM applied to complexes in 2D and to triangulated surfaces in 3D. The applet displays the gradient field, the critical simplices, the critical components and the Pareto set. It reproduces the examples of the paper published in Topological Methods in Nonlinear Analysis.

Open

Neighbour selection: k-NN, MMR and RNG-Score

Comparison of methods for selecting points around a query point, in two or three dimensions, with the Voronoi diagram and proximity graphs. The applet accompanies the preprint on relevance and diversity in information retrieval.

Open

Persistence diagram of a point cloud

The Vietoris–Rips complex of a point cloud in the plane or in space at an adjustable scale and the corresponding persistence diagram.

Open

Nearest neighbours and choice of distance

The k nearest neighbours of a query point among the points of a dataset. The point cloud may be 2D or 3D and the Euclidean, Manhattan, Chebyshev and cosine distances are implemented.
