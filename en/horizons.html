The seminar

Horizons Mathématiques is a mathematics seminar held in the Département de mathématiques et d'informatique of UQTR since winter 2026. Its objective is to introduce undergraduate students to current research topics by presenting them in an accessible way. The talks cover subjects that are not addressed in undergraduate courses and they are open to everyone. They are given in French.

I co-organize the seminar with Pierre-Olivier Parisé, professor at UQTR, and Mathieu Cloutier, former student at UQTR and now a master's student at UQAM. Most talks are recorded and posted on the YouTube channel of the seminar.

An introduction to homotopy

In February 2026, I gave the talk "Les chandails ont trois trous : une introduction à l'homotopie" (Shirts have three holes: an introduction to homotopy). Using visual examples, I explained when two spaces have the same homotopy type and how this notion makes it possible to simplify a cellular complex by collapsing pairs of cells. Following this idea, we saw that a shirt can be reduced to a wedge sum of three circles.
