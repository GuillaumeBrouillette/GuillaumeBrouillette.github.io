Course descriptions and contributions

Découvertes mathématiques (PMA1007)

This undergraduate course has three parts: combinatorics, the computer algebra system Maple and a term project. My lecture notes Introduction à Maple (in French) cover symbolic expressions, programming, graphs of functions, animations and three-dimensional graphical objects. They are based on the notes and the textbook of Alain Goupil.

For the term project, each team presents a mathematical topic to the group through a talk, Maple programs and a written report. The topics covered include GPS receivers, cartography, cycloids, Bézier curves, fractals and RSA cryptography.

Preuves et démonstrations mathématiques (PMA1006)

This course is intended for future high school mathematics teachers. It covers proof methods as well as the teaching of proof. The term includes two assignments. In the first one, each team analyzes a text on the concept of proof or on its teaching. In the second one, each team designs a teaching activity for high school students and leads part of it in front of the group.

Éléments d'analyse (MPU1049)

This course is intended for future high school mathematics teachers. It covers natural, integer, rational, real and complex numbers, the topology of the real line and of the plane, sequences and series as well as limits and continuity of real functions. At the beginning of the term, we review the properties of integers and real numbers before moving on to analysis itself.

Since the course contains a lot of theory, I took the initiative to set up weekly online quizzes, each covering the previous class, in order to encourage the group to review newly seen concepts on a regular basis.

Supervision

In fall 2026, I supervise a computer science student from Cégep de Thetford as part of the ExploraScience program. The project concerns computational topology and the implementation of topological tools in a database.
