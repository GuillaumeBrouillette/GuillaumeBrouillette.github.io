Welcome!

I am a postdoctoral researcher and lecturer in the Département de mathématiques et d'informatique of Université du Québec à Trois-Rivières (UQTR). My main research focus is in computational topology. More specifically, I use combinatorial structures to describe and compute the shape of a space or of a dataset.

My work concerns multiparameter discrete Morse theory, which extends the discrete Morse theory of Forman to vector-valued functions. I developed this theory during my PhD at Université de Sherbrooke under the supervision of Tomasz Kaczynski and Madjid Allili. In addition, my postdoctoral work applies geometric and topological methods to machine learning, notably to the reconstruction of fragmented objects and to information retrieval for large language models.

Research interests

  • Computational topology
  • Discrete Morse theory and multiparameter persistent homology
  • Topological data analysis
  • Machine learning: computer vision and large language models
